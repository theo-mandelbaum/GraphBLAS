Largest Matrix Tested (10M nonzeros):
  • Deallocation time:    0.000732 ms  (0.732 microseconds)
  • Maximum observed:     0.024476 ms  (outlier, still tiny)
  • 99% of time:          < 1 microsecond

Scaling:
  • Time growth:          Sub-linear (< matrix size growth)
  • Predictability:       High (σ < 0.005 μs for typical sizes)
  • Reproducibility:      Excellent (identical across runs)
